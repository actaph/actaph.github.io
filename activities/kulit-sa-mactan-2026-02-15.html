FEBRUARY 15, 2026

Kulit sa Mactan Festival 2026

ACTA members joined the Kulit sa Mactan Festival 2026 in Lapu-Lapu City as force multipliers, supporting Lapu-Lapu City police and traffic personnel. Members provided crowd and traffic assistance along the street-dancing route by day and around the festival grounds during the night program, then closed the duty with a team meal.
