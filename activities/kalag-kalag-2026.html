NOVEMBER 1–2, 2026

Kalag-kalag 2026 Cemetery Deployment

For Kalag-kalag 2026 (All Saints’ and All Souls’ Day), the ACTA team was assigned to the Lapu-Lapu City Public Cemetery in Humay-Humay, Lapu-Lapu City, working alongside Lapu-Lapu City Police Office personnel at the police assistance desk. Led by Founder Gov. Benjamin Maceren, members helped with crowd and traffic assistance, visitor help and roving presence through the day and into the night as families visited their loved ones.
