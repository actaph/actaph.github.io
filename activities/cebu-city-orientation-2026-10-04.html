OCTOBER 4, 2026

Cebu City Chapter Orientation

Applicants of the new ACTA Cebu City Chapter attended their membership orientation on October 4, 2026, from 2:00 to 5:00 PM at the 3rd floor of the Bulacao, Pardo Barangay Hall, Cebu City. The orientation was led by ACTA Founder Gov. Benjamin Maceren, together with Simeon “Buddy” Romarate and Councilor PCol. Paul Labra, ACTA BOD Frans Berdon and ACTA members from other chapters.
