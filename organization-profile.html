About ACTA

ACTA is a volunteer force multiplier for law enforcement. Members work alongside police and local government units on crime deterrence, traffic assistance and public-safety deployments during major community events.

ACTA exists because crime, traffic disorder, violence and illegal drugs remain everyday concerns in Cebu communities. The organization gives civic-minded residents a disciplined, uniformed and accountable way to help address them.

Vision, Mission and Core Values

Vision. A safer, more secure and harmonious Philippines where citizens and law enforcement work together to prevent crime and promote public safety — building trust, mutual respect and collective responsibility for the well-being of all.

Mission. To empower citizens to contribute to public safety by partnering with law enforcement agencies, promoting community engagement, and fostering a culture of vigilance and cooperation to prevent crime and address traffic issues.

Membership, Conduct and Uniforms

Membership is open to individuals committed to public safety, traffic assistance and community service. Applicants join in four steps:

  1. Inquire with a chapter officer
  2. Attend member orientation
  3. Screening and documentation
  4. Sworn in as an ACTA member

The online Members Portal lets the public verify a member’s ID and apply, and gives members a digital ID and chapter resources.

Code of Conduct. Members are bound by a written code covering honesty and integrity (falsification, fraud, forgery, conflicts of interest) and insubordination (disrespect of officers, non-cooperation with investigations). Penalties range from a written warning to expulsion.

Uniforms. Members deploy in official ACTA gear: the Type A and Type B shirts and a high-visibility vest with name and ID patch, so they are easy for the public and police to identify.

Partnership and Contact

ACTA welcomes partnerships with police units, LGUs, barangays, schools and private organizations on traffic assistance, event security support and community safety programs.
